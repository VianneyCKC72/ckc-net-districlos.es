How does Club Pro work?

Your Club Pro status depends on the total of your orders over the last 12 rolling months, for web customers only:

-5%
for any order from €0 to €2,500 excl. VAT

-7%
for any order from €2,500 to €5,000 excl. VAT

-10%
for any order over €5,000 excl. VAT

Each new order is added to your total, and orders older than one year gradually drop out: the more regularly you order, the more your status is maintained or improved. The discount matching your status is applied automatically, with no action required on your part.
